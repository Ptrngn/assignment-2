  Helping With My Family's Agriculture Business

  What was it that you did?
I helped with my family's business in western Vietnam, which sells pesticides and agricultural products and runs a drone spraying team. I'm now planning a mobile app to manage sales, inventory and drone spray data.

  When did you do it?
Growing up around the business and more seriously in recent years.

  What was good about it?
It showed me how a real business serves farmers, and it gives me a real problem I can solve with design and technology.

  What was your favourite memory of it?
Seeing the drone team spray fields and realising how much technology is changing farming back home.

  What didn't you enjoy about it?
Working across time zones and dealing with old systems that make tracking information difficult.
