  Working in a Restaurant

  What was it that you did?
I worked in food service at a restaurant, serving customers, keeping the front running smoothly and helping train new staff during busy shifts.

  When did you do it?
On and off over the past few years while studying.

  What was good about it?
It taught me how to stay calm under pressure and communicate quickly with customers and coworkers. It also showed me how a service actually runs behind the scenes, which connects directly to my Service Design studies.

  What was your favourite memory of it?
Getting through a packed weekend rush where the whole team was in sync and we ended the night joking around while cleaning up.

  What didn't you enjoy about it?
Long shifts, unclear scheduling and dealing with workplace boundary issues with management.

C – It was ok
