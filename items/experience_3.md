  Gym Training

  What was it that you did?
I train at the gym consistently, pushing sets to failure, tracking my protein and walking around 10,000 steps a day.

  When did you do it?
Ongoing, training most mornings.

  What was good about it?
It built discipline and routine. Seeing progress in the mirror and in my lifts proves that consistent effort pays off.

  What was your favourite memory of it?
The first time I noticed real visible change after sticking to my higher protein intake and training plan.

  What didn't you enjoy about it?
Plateaus, sore days, and having to eat a lot of protein even when I'm not hungry.
